Rough on purpose.

This is our first pass at how the site could be structured, built straight from the kickoff call, Eric's audit, and everything you shared with us. Boxes and labels, not colors and fonts. Think of it as us thinking out loud.

Some things in here we feel pretty confident about based on the data. Others are genuinely TBD and we need your brain on them. Either way, nothing is precious. If something feels off, tell us.

Let's use this as a conversation starter and go from here.
